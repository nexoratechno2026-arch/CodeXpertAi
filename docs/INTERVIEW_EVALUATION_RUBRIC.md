# Nexora InterviewAI — Evaluation Rubrics & Scoring Standards

## 1. Philosophy & Disclaimers

> [!CAUTION]
> AI-assisted scoring is designed as an educational practice diagnostic tool. It **does NOT** represent an official psychometric exam, an objective measurement of human aptitude, or a guarantee of employment placement.

Any numeric score (0–100) generated by InterviewAI is grounded in verifiable evidence from the candidate's actual written text, evaluated against clear rubric dimensions.

---

## 2. Category Rubrics

### A. Technical & Computer Science Core
- **Technical Correctness (40%)**: Accuracy of computational terminology (e.g., memory models, caching hierarchies, protocol semantics).
- **Reasoning & Trade-Offs (35%)**: Awareness of time vs. space complexity, CAP theorem implications, throughput vs. latency trade-offs.
- **Edge Cases & Failure Modes (25%)**: Consideration of network partitions, crash recovery, concurrency races.

### B. Programming & Algorithms
- **Algorithm Correctness (40%)**: State transition accuracy, base case handling, loop termination guarantees.
- **Complexity Analysis (35%)**: Big-O time and auxiliary space derivation.
- **Code Structure & Readability (25%)**: Decomposition into helper functions, idiomatic naming conventions.

### C. SQL & Database Architecture
- **Schema & Query Soundness (40%)**: Correct filtering (`WHERE`), grouping (`GROUP BY / HAVING`), and joins (`INNER / LEFT / CROSS`).
- **Performance & Indexing (35%)**: B-Tree mechanics, composite index ordering, query cost (`EXPLAIN`).
- **ACID & Concurrency (25%)**: Isolation levels, MVCC phenomena (dirty reads, phantom reads), lock escalation.

### D. HR & Cultural Leadership
- **Relevance & Directness (35%)**: Candid, prompt engagement with the question context.
- **Communication & Professional Maturity (35%)**: Constructive conflict resolution, self-awareness, active listening mindset.
- **Growth & Ownership (30%)**: Clear reflection on past failures, willingness to learn, collaborative attitude.

### E. Behavioral & Scenario-Based (STAR Method)
- **Situation & Task (25%)**: Concise setup of the engineering context and objective.
- **Action (40%)**: Personal contributions, specific decisions made under ambiguity.
- **Result & Learning (35%)**: Measurable business or engineering outcomes, retrospective takeaways.

## 3. Performance Tiers

| Tier | Score Range | Criteria | Next Steps |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Exceptional** | 85 – 100 | Precise terminology, robust trade-off reasoning, and structured STAR delivery. | Advance to Expert difficulty; explore system design. |
| **Strong** | 70 – 84 | Solid conceptual foundation; occasional omission of boundary edge cases. | Review distributed consensus and deeper performance tuning. |
| **Developing** | 50 – 69 | General understanding evident, but lacks concrete metrics or technical trade-offs. | Practice relevant topic problems in Nexora CodeLab. |
| **Needs Practice** | 0 – 49 | Superficial or truncated responses; missed core computational principles. | Complete foundational CS modules and re-attempt baseline questions. |
